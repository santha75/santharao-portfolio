◆ Capabilities

How I work.

From first research through production front-end — I don't hand off a picture, I hand off a system.

01 · Understand

Interrogate the domain

Shadow clinicians, ops leads, developers. Map where trust breaks — that's where design earns its keep.

02 · Structure

Architect the information

Flows, hierarchy and system tokens before any visual polish. Data-dense doesn't have to mean dense.

03 · Prototype

Test against reality

Usability sessions with real tasks and real data. AI outputs get tested for the failure case, not the demo case.

04 · Ship

Hand off in code

Production HTML/CSS and token specs, not redlines. Design QA until what ships matches what was designed.

◆ FAQ

Questions I get asked.

Senior, Lead and Principal UI/UX Designer roles at AI-first and enterprise-SaaS companies — ideally where design owns outcomes, not just screens. Open to full-time and select consulting.

I design in Figma and then implement production HTML/CSS (Tailwind or Bootstrap) myself. Handoffs arrive already validated in the browser, edge cases get caught early, and engineering starts from something real — which cut design-to-dev turnaround by ~20–25% on the teams I've led.

Most of my recent work is AI decision-support: human-in-the-loop review, automated actions and recommendations turned into dashboards experts can actually trust — at Brightcone.ai, and in clinical decision support for InpharmD's Formulary IQ.

Research and flows first, surface last. I map the single decision each screen exists to serve, prototype, test with real users, then build the system — tokens and components — so it scales past the first screen.

I'm based in Hyderabad and work comfortably with distributed teams across time zones. Remote-first is ideal; I'll consider relocation for the right team.

Yes — a deeper Brightcone.ai case study is in the works, and there's more on my Behance. Happy to walk through project specifics and NDA-safe details on a call.
