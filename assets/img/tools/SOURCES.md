# Tool logo sources

Original brand artwork, stored locally for the career tool lists and the “what I do” capability tiles. The ChatGPT marks have their viewBox cropped to the artwork (95 95 421 421) so they sit at the same optical size as the others. Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners. SVG geometry and brand colors are preserved. Iconify entries are SVG Porn logos (CC0-1.0); SVGL license is included in this directory.
