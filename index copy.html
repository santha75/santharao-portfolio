Hospitality · Cloud PMS

InnPro

Hotel operations, mobile

The system a hotel group actually runs on — the front desk, the owner and the outsourced laundry each working off one live inventory, from a phone instead of a back-office terminal.

Problem
Front-desk work happens standing up, mid-sentence with a guest. And the manager, the owner and the laundry contractor all need the same booking — filtered to what they’re allowed to see and touch. One forty-column desktop grid serves none of them.
Move
Split it into three role-scoped apps over one shared shell and data model. The manager gets a colour-coded room board and a check-in that fits on one screen; the owner gets the group’s occupancy and profit analysis; the vendor gets only pickups, returns and receivables. Colour carries state everywhere, so a room, an invoice and a leave request all read their status before you read the label.
  • 3role-scoped apps, one system
  • 80+screens designed end-to-end
  • 7operations modules on the floor

Workforce · HRM, Projects & Sales

PieTrack

now SpiralDeck

An all-in-one business suite on a phone — HR, sales, projects, marketing and SEO for one company or twenty, with team chat built into the same shell.

Problem
A suite this wide usually collapses into a launcher: six modules, six navigation models, and a consultant juggling three client companies re-learning the app every time they switch context.
Move
Made the company the top-level switch rather than a setting buried in the profile, then gave every module the same three layers — summary dashboard, list, detail sheet. Chat lives on the same tab bar as the work instead of in a second app, so a question about a leave request stays next to the leave request.

◆ Capabilities

How I work.

From first research through production front-end — I don't hand off a picture, I hand off a system.

01 · Understand

Interrogate the domain

Shadow clinicians, ops leads, developers. Map where trust breaks — that's where design earns its keep.

02 · Structure

Architect the information

Flows, hierarchy and system tokens before any visual polish. Data-dense doesn't have to mean dense.

03 · Prototype

Test against reality

Usability sessions with real tasks and real data. AI outputs get tested for the failure case, not the demo case.

04 · Ship

Hand off in code

Production HTML/CSS and token specs, not redlines. Design QA until what ships matches what was designed.
